Privacy

This page describes how Smart Site at smartsite.cloud handles information. Smart Site is an Empressa product operated by Legacy Group ATX LLC. It is a product description of how the service handles information, not legal advice.

What the product is

Smart Site is public-record property intelligence. Parcel facts, flood, land use, and related analysis come from public sources. We do not take privileged or relationship-gated county data. A path that only works because of a private relationship is not part of this product.

Accounts and sign-in

You sign in with Google or Microsoft. The app talks to those providers directly and receives the identity they return for that sign-in: a provider account id, and typically your name and email, so we can open or resume your Smart Site account. Smart Site has no password of its own and never receives one. The same account is used on the workbench and on the agent connector; connecting an AI client adds one OAuth step handled by WorkOS, which is not part of signing in to the app itself.

Billing

Paid plans are billed through Stripe. Stripe processes the payment method. We keep the subscription tier Stripe reports for your account (Free, Solo, Studio, or Team) so the workbench and the connector apply the same ceiling.

Agent connector

If you connect an AI client, that client talks to the Smart Site MCP server at mcp.smartsite.cloud. Access uses OAuth against the same Smart Site account. The connector is not the Hauska catalog at mcp.hauska.dev.

What we store

We store account identity from the sign-in, saved properties, shares you create, briefs and reports you run, and billing status from Stripe. A signed-in session uses a cookie on smartsite.cloud so the workbench can stay signed in.

A saved property carries everything you attached to it: your notes, your research threads and their summary, anything you drew on the map, and a record of the exports you ran for that place.

What never reaches our servers

  • Files you attach to a research thread stay in your browser. Only the text your browser could read out of a file is sent along with your question, so the assistant can reason about it. The file itself is never uploaded and we do not store it.
  • Card numbers. Stripe holds the payment method. We keep the Stripe customer reference and the plan tier, and nothing else about payment.
  • A transcript of your research chat. Your question and the answer are not written to our database.

Composing an answer does send your question, the property records it needs, and any text read out of a file you attached, to a third-party AI model provider. That is the one place it goes.

Shares you create

A share you mint is a grant you chose to publish. Pasting a share URL into a chat logs that URL. Do not treat a pasted link as confined to that chat. A share link stops working 30 days after you create it. There is no revoke control in the product today; a link can be revoked on request. See the product terms for use of the service.

Anonymous browsing

Before you sign in, the app keeps a random id in your browser and records what you did against it: that a browse started, which parcel you opened, and, when you search by address rather than tapping the map, the text you typed. That id is not linked to any account, and it goes away if you clear your browser storage. The records already written stay; see below.

How long we keep things

This section describes what the system does today, not what we intend it to do. Where nothing removes a record, this page says so rather than naming a retention period we do not enforce. An unenforced promise would be worse than no promise.

What you can remove yourself. Removing a saved property deletes it, along with the notes, research threads, chat summary, drawings and export record stored on it. That is a real delete, not a hidden flag. Removing a team member or an invitation deletes that row too. Signing out clears the session cookie in your browser.

What stops working on its own. A share link stops resolving 30 days after it is created. A signed-in session lasts 7 days, after which you sign in again. Both are enforced when the link or the session is used. Neither deletes anything: the share grant record remains in our database after the link stops working.

What we keep until you ask us to remove it. Everything else, for as long as the account exists. That means your account identity, plan tier and Stripe customer reference, your screens and the properties on them, the list of AI clients that have connected, the per-property free-message counters, any records request you ran together with the documents it retrieved, and the anonymous browse records described above. We do not run a job that deletes any of this on a schedule, and we would rather say so than name a period nothing enforces.

Deleting your account. There is no delete-my-account button in the product today. Email support@empressa.io and we will remove your account. Removing the account record automatically removes, by a database constraint, your sign-in identity, your plan and Stripe reference, your share grants, the record of connected AI clients, your chat counters and your team membership. It does not automatically remove your saved properties or your screens, which are keyed to your account but not linked to it by a constraint; those are removed separately as part of the same request. The anonymous browse records described above carry no account link and cannot be matched back to you, so they are not part of an account deletion.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell a public-record dump as the product.
  • We do not claim privileged county access.
  • We do not invent setbacks, flood zones, or zoning that is not on file.
