Privacy

This page describes how Smart Site at smartsite.cloud handles information. Smart Site is an Empressa product operated by Legacy Group ATX LLC. It is a product description of how the service handles information, not legal advice.

What the product is

Smart Site is public-record property intelligence. Parcel facts, flood, land use, and related analysis come from public sources. We do not take privileged or relationship-gated county data. A path that only works because of a private relationship is not part of this product.

Accounts and sign-in

You sign in with Google or Microsoft through WorkOS AuthKit. WorkOS returns the identity those providers share for that sign-in, typically name and email, so we can open or resume your Smart Site account. The same sign-in is used on the workbench and on the agent connector.

Billing

Paid plans are billed through Stripe. Stripe processes the payment method. We keep the subscription tier Stripe reports for your account (Free, Solo, Studio, or Team) so the workbench and the connector apply the same ceiling.

Agent connector

If you connect an AI client, that client talks to the Smart Site MCP server at mcp.smartsite.cloud. Access uses OAuth against the same Smart Site account. The connector is not the Hauska catalog at mcp.hauska.dev.

What we store

We store account identity from the sign-in, saved properties, shares you create, briefs and reports you run, and billing status from Stripe. A signed-in session uses a cookie on smartsite.cloud so the workbench can stay signed in.

Shares you create

A share you mint is a grant you chose to publish. Pasting a share URL into a chat logs that URL. Do not treat a pasted link as confined to that chat. Grants expire. See the product terms for use of the service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell a public-record dump as the product.
  • We do not claim privileged county access.
  • We do not invent setbacks, flood zones, or zoning that is not on file.
