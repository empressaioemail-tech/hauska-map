These terms cover use of Smart Site at smartsite.cloud and the Smart Site MCP server at mcp.smartsite.cloud. Smart Site is an Empressa product operated by Legacy Group ATX LLC. This page is a product description of how the service works, not legal advice.

The service

Smart Site is public-record property intelligence for land research. You may use the workbench and, if you connect one, an AI client against the same account. Analysis is not a survey, a title opinion, or a legal determination. Where a fact is not on file, the product says so rather than inventing a number.

Accounts

Account features require sign-in with Google or Microsoft through WorkOS AuthKit. You are responsible for the account those providers authenticate. We may suspend an account that abuses the service or tries to pull privileged data the product does not carry.

Plans and billing

Access follows your Stripe plan: Free, Solo, Studio, or Team. The same ceiling applies on the workbench and on the connector. Stripe processes payment. You can cancel a paid plan through the Stripe billing flow in the product. Features above your tier refuse; they do not silently downgrade into a thinner answer presented as complete.

Agent access

Connecting a client to mcp.smartsite.cloud lets that client act as you at your current tier. Unresolvable or revoked credentials fail closed. The connector is Smart Site, not the Hauska catalog.

Shares

A share you create is a grant you chose to publish. Anyone with the link can fetch it until it expires or you revoke it. Pasting the URL into a chat logs the URL.

Public record only

We do not take privileged or relationship-gated county data. Do not upload material you are not allowed to share. Do not use the service to claim a relationship with a jurisdiction you do not have.
